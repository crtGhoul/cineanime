✨ How CineAnime Operates

Designed with privacy-first principles and decentralized media aggregation.

⚖️ Terms of Use & Legal Compliance Disclosures

Safe Harbor, Non-Hosting & DMCA Compliance

1. General Architecture & Non-Hosting Notice:
CineAnime operates solely as a client-side media browser, catalog aggregator, and user-interface layer (analogous to popular open-source media centers such as Kodi, Jellyfin, and Stremio). CineAnime does not host, upload, scrape, store, transmit, or manage any copyrighted media, video files, or digital streams on any server. All application source files consist solely of static client-side JavaScript, CSS, and HTML executed entirely inside the end-user's local environment.

2. Metadata & Public API Integration:
All media titles, descriptions, cast listings, episode titles, and poster artwork displayed within this application are queried directly from publicly accessible third-party APIs (including AniList GraphQL, Kitsu Open API, Cinemeta, and AniSkip). CineAnime holds no affiliation with these independent services.

3. Third-Party Endpoints & User Discretion:
Any video playback functionality is established either through direct user-configured protocols (such as community Stremio add-on URLs, local network DLNA/HLS shares) or independent third-party embed links. CineAnime exercises no administrative control over external domains, their contents, or their availability. End-users are solely responsible for ensuring that their consumption of media complies with all local copyright and telecommunications regulations in their respective jurisdictions.

4. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) / Copyright Inquiries:
Because CineAnime stores zero media assets, removing this software or its repositories will not remove external streams hosted by third parties.
